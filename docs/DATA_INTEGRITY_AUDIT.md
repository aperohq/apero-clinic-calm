## Live integrity and access evidence

- One clinic row: Dental Zone Clinic. One owner profile; no live manager or staff user account was available for behavioral RLS tests.
- Counts: 7 clients, 4 staff, 28 weekly availability rows, 0 time-off rows, 8 appointments, 22 conversations, 1 client note, 0 voice requests.
- All 14 inspected clinic and voice tables have RLS enabled. Policies scope clinic-owned reads to `current_clinic_id()`; staff availability/time off writes require owner or manager. Profile update is own-user only, with a sensitive-field guard. Service role retains needed table access.
- Live same-clinic reference triggers cover appointments, conversations, notes, notifications and availability; time off has a composite clinic/staff foreign key and guard. No cross-clinic mismatches or duplicate staff/day rows were found. Appointment staff overlaps are excluded by a GiST constraint.
- No invalid appointment ranges or null appointment client/staff references were found. Fifteen conversations have no client association, 17 have no appointment association, five have no recording, and four have no transcript. These may be legitimate unmatched/incomplete calls and need clinic review, not automatic repair.
- `db/01_phase2_schema.sql` development seed IDs remain live. The owner confirmed test operational records are expected and will be cleaned immediately before handoff; they are not a launch blocker. Preserve the clinic, real staff, services, working hours, time off, and voice configuration. No data was deleted.
- On 2026-09-18 the owner manually applied migration 05. Live `voice_integrations` and `clinic_service_aliases`, the integration-aware `voice_apply_appointment`, and clinic ownership guards were verified. All four current Edge Functions were deployed and signed routing checks passed. The draft 07 duplicated 05 and was removed without applying it. The published flow version 2 still sends all 10 tools to `clinic-operations`; see `docs/VOICE_RECONCILIATION.md`.
- RLS policy inspection and reference constraints support tenant isolation, but with only one live clinic/profile, cross-tenant behavior was not exercised with real user sessions. A second-clinic fixture test is required before general multi-clinic launch.
